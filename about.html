The Vault Origin

WHY WE FOUNDED IRONVAULT

For decades, the fitness supplement market was plagued with misleading marketing tricks: proprietary blends hiding underdosed actives, cheap amino-spiking inflating perceived protein ratios, and extravagant celebrity endorsements concealing poor manufacturing standards.

We started IRONVAULT with a singular principle: if it doesn't belong in an elite athlete's biology, it has zero place on our shelves. We partner directly with verified global manufacturers to curate sports nutrition organized strictly around authentic training demands.

Core Principles

THE FOUR IRON PILLARS

The foundational standards governing every formula selected for the IRONVAULT store.

PILLAR 01

100% Label Transparency

We strictly ban hidden blends and unverified "filler matrixes". Athletes have the right to know exact active ingredient quantities per scoop.

PILLAR 02

Zero Amino-Spiking

Total protein metrics are derived purely from complete dairy or plant fractions. We reject powders inflated with cheap glycine or free-form taurine.

PILLAR 03

Clinical Dosages

Key training compounds like Creapure® Creatine, CarnoSyn® Beta-Alanine, and L-Citrulline are vetted for scientifically validated ergogenic dosages.

PILLAR 04

Climate-Controlled Care

Supplements are temperature and humidity sensitive. Our logistics center preserves bioactive peptide integrity through monitored storage.

Quality Control Protocol

HOW WE VET EVERY TUB

Before a supplement is listed in IRONVAULT, it must pass a rigorous 3-stage validation process conducted in accordance with international sports nutrition benchmarks.

1
Certificate of Analysis (COA) Audit

Every incoming production lot is verified for microbiological purity, heavy metals, and HPLC protein concentration.

2
Distributor Authorization Check

We work directly with authorized brand distribution channels to prevent counterfeit merchandise or altered sealings.

3
Tamper-Evident Physical Inspection

Inner pressure-sealed foil and exterior safety shrink-wraps are examined before final warehouse stocking.

VERIFICATION MATRIX STANDARD 2026
  • Protein Purity by Kjeldahl/HPLC Minimum 80% on Isolates
  • Banned Substance Screening WADA / Informed-Choice Standards
  • Heavy Metal Screening (Pb, As, Cd, Hg) < USP Safety Thresholds
  • Solubility & Dispersion Index Instantized • Zero Clumping
  • Batch Integrity Seal Induction Heat Sealed
